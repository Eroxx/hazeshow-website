Hazeshow · For Mac and Apple TV

A live light show for whatever you're playing.

Hazeshow listens to the music on your Mac and lights a stage for it, beat by beat.

Coming to the App Store

Play anything: Spotify, Apple Music, a video, a record on the turntable. Hazeshow hears the beat, finds the downbeat, knows when the chorus is coming, and runs the lights the way a lighting designer would.

Room 01

The arena

Trusses of moving lights that sweep, fan and chase with the band, the way the big touring rigs do. Five venues, from a small theatre to a stadium.

Room 02

The jazz club

Piano, bass, drums and sax under warm lamps, candles on the tables, and a camera that finds each instrument in turn.

Room 03

The concert hall

An orchestra's instruments on stage at the interval. The light follows the music's swells and colour, not its beat.

Room 04

The club

An LED wall, lasers over the crowd and a dance floor that runs with the track, going dark before the drop.

Visuals

Six more ways to see a song

Pictures painted by the music itself, from a 1960s liquid light show to a galaxy whose stars are the notes.

Apple TV

Made for the big screen.

Send the show to your Apple TV from your Mac, and let the TV play the music too, in time with its lights.

Any source

Play from your iPhone or iPad to your Mac with AirPlay; the show and the music come out of the TV together.

Smooth

Rendered live on the Apple TV at 60 frames a second.

Your way on the Mac

In a window, full screen, floating over your work, or behind your desktop icons.

How it listens

It really listens.

The downbeat

Changes land on the 1, the way a lighting designer calls a cue.

What's coming

Play a song file or turn on Look ahead and it hears seconds ahead, holding back before a drop and landing on it.

The song's shape

Verses, choruses and solos, from the sound and the song's lyrics. Every chorus goes bigger than the last.

The right room

Rock gets the arena, jazz the club, a symphony the hall, from each song's genre. Or choose.

Songs it has heard

A recording it knows gets its saved show again, from any app.

Your taste

Press J where you wanted a change. It changes there every time, and learns how you like it.
