Hazeshow

A live light show for your music.

Play anything (Spotify, Apple Music, a video, even a record) and Hazeshow turns it into a concert you can watch, lit with the music the way a real lighting designer would. 25 stages, from a small theater to a stadium with fireworks to a drone show over a lake, and a new light show for every song you play.

25 stages, more on the way

25 stages. Endless light shows.

Thirteen for concerts and twelve visual worlds. Hazeshow picks the stage that suits each song, or you choose, or let it shuffle. Then the song itself runs the show, with its own colors, shapes and moves, so no two songs look alike. New stages arrive with updates.

Concert venues

One big rock show, five places to see it.

Rows of moving lights that sweep and chase with the band, and a camera that cuts with the music like a concert film. Each venue has its own moment.

Stages for each kind of music

The right room for the song.

A solo guitar gets the small room, a symphony the concert hall, a drop the club. Eight more stages, each lit the way that music is lit live.

Visuals

Or watch the music paint pictures.

Twelve visuals drawn by the song as it plays: every note becomes a star, a drop of ink, a brush stroke, a laser, a jet of water or a drone in the sky.

Apple TV

Best on the big screen.

Tap once to send the show from your iPhone, iPad or Mac to your Apple TV. The TV can play the music too, perfectly in time with the lights. Turn off the lamps and it's a concert in your living room.

From your phone

Your iPhone or iPad listens to the music playing in the room and runs the show on the TV. No Mac needed.

Smooth and sharp

The Apple TV draws the show itself, live and silky smooth.

Your way on the Mac

Full screen, in a small window over your work, behind your desktop icons, or as a screen saver that dances to whatever's playing.

How it listens

It really listens to the music.

On the beat

Big changes land on the first beat of the bar, just where you'd nod your head. Turn it smoother for background, or punchier for a party.

What's coming

Play a song file, or a song it has heard before, and it knows where the drop is, so it holds back and lands right on it.

The whole song

It knows a verse from a chorus, and every chorus goes bigger than the last.

The right stage

It picks the stage that suits each song: a solo guitar gets the small room, a symphony the concert hall. Or choose your own, or shuffle.

Songs it knows

Play a song again and it remembers the show it made for it.

On time in your room

On iPhone and iPad it plays a beat and times its own lights against it, so they land on the beat wherever you are, AirPods and all.

Replay

Save the moment.

The chorus just hit and the lights went huge? Replay saves the last 30 seconds of the show, with its sound, as a video, already copied so you can paste it straight into a message. Or start recording and catch the next 30.
