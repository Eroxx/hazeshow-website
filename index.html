Hazeshow · For iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV

A live light show for your music.

Play anything (Spotify, Apple Music, a video, even a record) and Hazeshow turns it into a concert you can watch: more than ten stages and visuals, lit on the beat the way a real lighting designer would.

Stages

A stage for every kind of song.

Hazeshow picks the stage that suits each song, or you choose. The camera moves too, from the back row to right up close, cutting with the music like a concert film.

Stage 01

Different venues

Rows of moving lights that sweep, fan out and chase with the band, like the big rock tours. Pick your venue: a small theater, an arena, an amphitheater, a stadium, or the Dome, where the show wraps round you and over your head on one giant screen.

Stage 02

The industrial stage

Bare steel, a wall of haze and screens, and hard white light cutting through it in clean, mechanical shapes. Built for heavy music: the lights hit like machines and the band stands in silhouette.

Stage 03

The club

A video wall, lasers over the crowd and a dance floor that lights up with the track. It goes dark before the drop, then everything hits at once.

Stage 04

The EDM stage

Built for big electronic anthems: a structure of LED panels that takes a new shape for every song, a video wall behind the decks and lasers fanning out over the crowd. One bold colour at a time, then two when the drop lands.

Stage 05

The jazz club

A small, warm room: piano, bass, drums and sax under soft lamps, candles on the tables, and a camera that drifts from one instrument to the next. It never flashes.

Stage 06

The concert hall

A grand stage set for an orchestra. Here the light rises and falls with the music's swells and moods rather than a beat.

Every song, a different show

No two songs look alike.

Each song gets its own colours, shapes and moves, on whichever stage suits it. A few of the looks from one evening's music.

Visuals

Or watch the music paint pictures

Six visuals drawn by the song as it plays: every note becomes a star, a flower, a drop of ink or a brush stroke, so no two songs look alike.

Apple TV

Best on the big screen.

Tap once to send the show from your iPhone, iPad or Mac to your Apple TV. The TV can play the music too, perfectly in time with the lights. Turn off the lamps and it's a concert in your living room.

From your phone

Your iPhone or iPad listens to the music playing in the room and runs the show on the TV. No Mac needed.

Smooth and sharp

The Apple TV draws the show itself, live and silky smooth.

Your way on the Mac

Full screen, in a small window over your work, behind your desktop icons, or as a screen saver that dances to whatever's playing.

How it listens

It really listens to the music.

On the beat

Big changes land on the first beat of the bar, just where you'd nod your head.

What's coming

It can hear a few seconds ahead, so it holds back before a drop and lands right on it.

The whole song

It knows a verse from a chorus, and every chorus goes bigger than the last.

The right stage

It picks the stage that suits each song's style. Or choose your own.

Songs it knows

Play a song again and it remembers the show it made for it.

On time in your room

On iPhone and iPad it plays a beat and times its own lights against it, so they land on the beat wherever you are, AirPods and all.
