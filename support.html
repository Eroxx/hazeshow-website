Support

Hazeshow lights a live show for your music, on your iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Here are answers to the usual questions.

Ask other Hazeshow users and me on the Discord: questions in #help, bugs in #bug-reports, wishes in #ideas.

Join the Discord

Still stuck? Write to support@hazeshow.com.

The lights aren't moving

Hazeshow needs to hear the music. On a Mac, the first time, macOS asks to let it hear your Mac's sound: allow it in System Settings, Privacy & Security, Screen & System Audio Recording. Then check Settings → Sound is set to the sound playing on this Mac.

On iPhone and iPad, Hazeshow listens through the microphone, so play the music out loud nearby; allow the microphone in Settings, Hazeshow if you turned it down. (iOS lets no app hear what other apps play, except through a screen broadcast: Hazeshow's Settings, Sound.)

No music handy?

Hazeshow comes with a song of its own, First Light. Play it from the welcome (Just show me), the Room light on iPhone and iPad, or ⇧D on a Mac: the show tours every room, a card naming each. On an Apple TV, choose Watch a show now and the TV puts it on by itself.

How do I get it on my TV?

Install Hazeshow on your Apple TV and open it, on the same Wi-Fi. On iPhone or iPad, tap the Apple TV light and choose your TV. On a Mac, choose it in Settings → Apple TV (or press D). Turn on Play the music on the TV to hear the music from the TV, in time with the lights.

Do I need a Mac?

No. Hazeshow on iPhone and iPad runs the show itself, and sends it to an Apple TV. On a Mac you can also send your iPhone's sound to the Mac with AirPlay (turn on AirPlay Receiver in System Settings, General, AirDrop & Handoff), and Hazeshow hears it like anything else the Mac plays.

Can it be my screen saver?

On a Mac, yes, one that follows the music: in Settings → Window, choose Show the music after a few minutes. With music playing and no mouse or keys for that long, the show fills the screen; touch anything to get your desktop back.

Shortcuts and Siri

Hazeshow's actions are in the Shortcuts app on iPhone, iPad and Mac: play the demo song, choose a room, next room, show on Apple TV. Ask Siri, for example, to “Show Hazeshow on the TV”.

The lights are a little behind the music

Bluetooth speakers and AirPods play late. Raise Delay the lights in Settings → Sound until they line up. For a TV, use TV lag in Settings → Apple TV.

How do I pick the room?

On iPhone and iPad, swipe across the show or tap the Room light. On a Mac, press G to step through the rooms and pictures, or choose in Settings → Show. Automatic picks from the song's genre; ⇧G brings it back to Automatic.

Can I teach it?

Press J where you wanted the lights to change. That song changes there every time it plays, and Hazeshow learns how often you like changes. Press ? to see every key.
