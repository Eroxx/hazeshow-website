Privacy

Hazeshow listens to music so it can light it. The listening happens on your device, and the sound never leaves it. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • The sound. On a Mac, Hazeshow analyses what the Mac is playing (or what its microphone hears, if you choose that). On iPhone and iPad, it analyses what the microphone hears, or, if you start a screen broadcast to it, that device's own sound (only the sound: the picture is ignored). It all happens as the music plays. Nothing is recorded or kept.
  • Lights Timing. On iPhone and iPad, timing the lights plays a short beat and listens for it through the microphone, to measure the delay. Nothing is recorded or kept.
  • What it remembers. For songs it has heard, it saves a short fingerprint and the show it made, so the same recording gets the same show next time. You can forget them all in Settings.
  • Your settings and favourites. Your favourites, timing, settings and any changes you mark are saved on the device you set them on.

What it asks the internet

To know what's playing and give it the right show, Hazeshow sends the artist and song name (never the sound) to these services:

  • Last.fm (ws.audioscrobbler.com): the artist and title, for the song's genre tags; on a Mac, also your Last.fm username, if you enter one, to see what you're playing. Their privacy policy.
  • LRCLIB (lrclib.net): the artist, title and length, to fetch the song's timed lyrics, which help it find verses and choruses.
  • Spotify's image server: on a Mac, if you play from Spotify and turn on album colours, Hazeshow downloads the cover picture Spotify reports for the song, to take its colours.

It reads what's playing from the Spotify and Music apps on a Mac, and from Apple Music on iPhone and iPad. Each asks your permission the first time.

Apple TV

When you show Hazeshow on your Apple TV, your iPhone, iPad or Mac sends the picture's instructions, and the music if you choose, straight to the TV over your home network. Nothing goes through the internet.

Buying

The purchase that unlocks your own music goes through Apple. Hazeshow never sees your payment details.
